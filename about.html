About Tegula Real Estate

Tegula Real properties is a leading real estate agency located in Uganda. With several years experience in the industry, the agency specializes in providing a wide range of real estate services, including properties for rent and properties for sale.


At Tegula real properties limited, our team is committed to providing clients with the highest level of service possibleWhether you are looking to buy or rent a property, the agency a team of experienced proffessionals who can guide you through the process from start to finish. They work closely with you to understand your needs and preferences, and will use their indepth knowledge of the local real estate Market to help you find the perfect property.


The agency prides itself on its strong relationships with the property owners and landlords in East Africa, which enables them to offer clients a wide range of properties to choose from. from cozy apartments, acquiring land to spacious family homes, the agency has something to suite everyone's needs and budget.


In addition to its sales and rental services, tegula properties also offers a range of property management services to help property owners and landlords Maximize their investment Whether you are looking to rent out property or want to acquire property, the agency has the entertise and resources to help.


Overall, Tegula real properties limited is a trusted and reliable real estate agency that offers a comphrehensive range of services to clients in kampala and beyond. With a strong track record of success and a commitment to delivering exceptional service in Uganda, Kenya and Tanzania, the agency is the ideal choice for anyone looking to buy, sell or rent property all around East Africa.
