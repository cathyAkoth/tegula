About Tegula Real Properties

Tegula Real Properties is a leading real estate agency located in Uganda. With several years of experience in the industry, the agency specializes in providing a wide range of real estate services, including properties for rent and properties for sale.

At Tegula Real Properties Limited, our team is committed to providing clients with the highest level of service possible. Whether you are looking to buy or rent a property, the agency has a team of experienced professionals who guide you through the process from start to finish. We work closely with clients to understand their needs and preferences and use our in-depth knowledge of the local real estate market to help them find the perfect property.

The agency prides itself on its strong relationships with property owners and landlords across East Africa, enabling us to offer a wide range of options. From cozy apartments and land acquisition to spacious family homes, we provide solutions to suit different needs and budgets.

In addition to sales and rental services, Tegula Real Properties offers professional property management services to help owners and landlords maximize their investment. Whether you are looking to rent out property or acquire property, the agency has the expertise and resources to support you.

Overall, Tegula Real Properties Limited is a trusted and reliable real estate agency serving clients in Kampala and beyond. With a strong track record and a commitment to delivering exceptional service in Uganda, Kenya, and Tanzania, we are the ideal choice for anyone looking to buy, sell, or rent property across East Africa.
